Word Chain Crossword Search

Read the crossword clue, find the word in the grid, and chain each new word from the last letter of the previous word!

How to Play

  1. Read the Crossword Clue and letter count below.
  2. Click and drag across letters in a straight line (up, down, left, or right) — or click the first letter, then click the last letter — to select your answer.
  3. After you find a word, its last letter glows dark pink. The next word in the chain starts directly on that glowing tile!
  4. Earn +100 points per word (+25 streak bonus for no mistakes). Wrong guesses cost -15 points, and hints cost -25 points.
